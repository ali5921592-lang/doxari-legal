Doxari Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

In short: Doxari keeps your document workspace on your device, asks before sending text to AI, and makes its advertising and Premium model clear.

Documents and device storage

Doxari manages the documents you choose, recent-document entries, favourites and preferences on your device. Camera access is requested only when you choose to scan a document. Doxari does not upload or share a document merely because you open, scan, merge, split or compress it.

AI Assistant

The AI Assistant processes only text you choose to paste or submit for a summary, explanation or translation. Before any text is submitted, Doxari requires a separate consent choice. That text is sent to an AI processing service solely to provide the requested result. Do not submit sensitive information or content you are not authorised to share.

Advertising and Premium

The free experience may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). This technology may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising data, app interactions and diagnostic or performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Premium subscribers do not see ads. Payments and subscriptions are managed by Apple App Store or Google Play; Doxari does not receive payment-card details.

Your choices

You can decide not to use the AI Assistant, leave its consent control unticked, clear recent-document history in Settings and change device permissions in your operating-system settings. Doxari does not currently require a user account.
